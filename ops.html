Prologue

Launches don't fail in the design. They fail in the coordination.

Engineering ships on time but the documentation is six commits behind. The marketing site goes live three days before the API actually works. Support hasn't been briefed. Dev rel finds out when a customer asks. The team did everything right inside its own lane — and the launch still missed, because the connective tissue wasn't owned by anyone.

Stealth Ops owns the connective tissue. It sits between engineering, design, content, and external partners on the launches that need brand, docs, and code coordinated. It lands the launch on a fixed date with intact scope. It writes the operating system the team keeps after the launch is over.

A back-door service. Built for warm conversations, not cold inbound. The page below is a capability sheet — not a pitch.

The Services

Five offers. One ladder.

01

Launch Readiness Diagnostic

A two-week assessment of an upcoming launch, release, or developer-facing rollout. Reviews the cross-functional plan across engineering, PM, DX, marketing, and support. Surfaces gaps in scope ownership, comms cadence, and post-launch monitoring. Outputs a remediation plan sized for either an internal fix or a Stealth Ops sprint.

Deliverables
  • 60-min kickoff plus read access to specs, plans, launch calendar, Slack
  • Diagnostic memo (8–12 pages) — RACI gaps, risk register, comms gaps, rollout checks
  • 30-min walkthrough with founder or engineering lead
  • Recommended next-step scope, sized for internal vs. external delivery
50% of the diagnostic fee credited toward any Stealth Ops sprint booked within 30 days.

02

Launch Coordination Sprint

Cross-functional coordination for a Series A launch where no internal program function exists yet. Stealth Ops sits between engineering, design, content, and external partners to land the launch on a fixed date with intact scope. Delivery shape modeled on the Stealth Strategist Culdesac engagement — multi-phase brand, web, intake, and live operations coordinated by one person.

Deliverables

  • Weekly cross-functional standups — run, not just attended
  • Asset and handoff tracker across design, copy, dev, docs, support
  • Risk log plus go/no-go checkpoint at T-7
  • Launch-week comms calendar, internal and external
  • Post-launch retrospective plus ritual handoff doc the team keeps

  • 30-day adoption check-in

Interview architecture for early-stage engineering and DX teams scaling hiring without a recruiting-ops function. Drawn from five years in technical recruiting: two years full-cycle at Kforce running technical hiring processes end-to-end, plus three years sourcing at Meta — engineering org structure, loop design, calibration. Not candidate sourcing; loop design and rubric calibration only.
